Three worlds. Every stop is a choice. Ice in your veins, or you're not making the boss.

THE RULES

Each fight, you're dealt a hand. Select cards and play them. The better the poker hand — Pair, Straight, Flush, Full House, all the way to Straight Flush — the bigger your multiplier. Clear the target across your plays before you run out.

Bad hand? Discard to redraw — limited uses per fight.

A flush (all played cards the same stance) triggers a bonus on top:

HEAVY — flat bonus damage
CREW — heals a life
SWIFT — burst bonus damage
FOCUS — shields your next loss

Miss the target and you take a hit. Lose all 3 lives and it's back to the start of the world. Along the way you'll hit forks — a tougher Elite fight for a guaranteed buff, or a Rest to heal — plus one personal detour per world with its own choice. Beat all 3 bosses to win the run.
